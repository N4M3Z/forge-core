ADRs live in `docs/decisions/` with the template at `templates/adr.md`. Use `templates/madr.md` for complex multi-stakeholder decisions. Frontmatter must include `status` and `date`.

Before proposing architectural changes or challenging existing patterns, check the project's ADRs via the ArchitectureDecisions skill. Do not contradict an accepted ADR without explicitly acknowledging it and proposing a superseding record.
