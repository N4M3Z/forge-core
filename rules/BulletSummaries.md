Default to bullet-point summaries, not prose paragraphs. When presenting findings, options, status, comparisons, recommendations, or any multi-item result, lead with tight bullets, one idea per bullet, and keep prose to a single framing sentence or a point that genuinely needs one.

Keep the bullets substantive: three real bullets beat seven (LessIsMore), and a bullet that only restates its heading should be cut (Deslop). Don't bullet a single thought, a one-line answer stays one line.
